Definitions and coverage

Public-transport passengers and teleported modes are not reconstructed as road drivers. Unique users exclude transit drivers and bikes. Employment and student status are not inferred from age or daily activities.

The French group is the external-France population. The separate cross-border-demand group also contains French residents; these are population-source categories, not nationality groups.

Home origins are aggregated to 2 km cells; up to 300 cells are shown. Freight/LCV users often have no exported home or age.
