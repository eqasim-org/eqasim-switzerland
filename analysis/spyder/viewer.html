Trip selection

Population groups refer to the vehicle driver.

Time refers to entry onto the selected link. End time is exclusive. Hours above 24 are supported.

Display

Default: motorway, trunk, primary, secondary, tertiary and their ramps. Spider flows draw above the background network.

Nested destinations use a darker shade. Combined views use the dominant direction color per link; hover shows both counts. Hidden road classes and minimum-flow filters can leave gaps.

Widths = 1 + maximum × (link trips / selected trips)exponent. Counts are simulated vehicle trips, without population expansion. Hover for counts.
